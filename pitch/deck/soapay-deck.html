Architecture

Three apps on open standards. Nothing of ours holds money.

Recipient app
Your keys, your ledger, your guard. Nothing leaves the browser.
Sender app
Paste names and amounts. One transaction pays everyone on fresh addresses.
Optional gateway
For wallets that cannot derive. Self-host it or use ours. The app checks its work.
Chain
ENS name. Stealth address registry and announcer. Gasless spend. Screened exit.
Every contract we touch is a public standard someone else already audited.
The only thing you ever share is a name.
Everything rebuilds from your seed with every server off.
